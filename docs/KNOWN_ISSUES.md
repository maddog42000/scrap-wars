# Known Issues — Current SCRAP WARS Milestone

This is an iterative development build, not the final production APK. See `TEST_REPORT.md` for what was actually exercised; headless scene startup is not equivalent to visual/mobile QA.

## Remaining GDD / production gaps

- **Android delivery and QA:** no APK was exported; no SDK/device testing was available. Installation, true handset landscape behavior, touch controls, process suspension/resume, offline restart, performance and Android-specific physics behavior are **Not tested**.
- **Visual production quality:** art is original, project-authored procedural development graphics and native Godot controls, but is not final commissioned/polished art. Rendered layouts, safe areas, aspect ratios, contrast and animation on-device have not been reviewed. No stock art is used.
- **Replacement art is not supplied yet:** the project now has documented canonical folders and runtime paths for chassis, locomotion, weapons, armor, utility modules, arenas, the menu background/feature image, and selected button skins. Existing procedural/native visuals remain as automatic fallbacks. The static PNG resolver, missing-file fallback, and SpriteFrames loader/mount were tested headlessly with temporary generated resources that were removed. Final replacement compositions, all named paths with authored art, animated timing/appearance, and rendered quality were **Not tested**. Button background animation is not implemented; buttons support base and per-state PNG skins. See `ART_ASSET_PIPELINE.md` for the exact paths and requirements.
- **Audio/VFX production scope:** core combat feedback now plays generated engine/rotary loops, metal impacts, rocket launch/blast sounds, and procedural CPU particles for exhaust, hit sparks/smoke, rocket trails/explosions and disabled parts. The full checklist is still incomplete: no menu/music/UI SFX, wheel/crank/booster-specific layers, hammer-swing/result cues, haptics, mastered mix, rendered particle QA, or audio-speaker/headset QA. SFX events/resources and loop starts were verified in headless Godot only.
- **Crank-leg locomotion is not production-ready:** the axle/leg are linked RigidBody2D bodies and the foot records real floor contacts, but the latest crank-focused physics run accumulated excessive rotation, traveled 6,403.7 px, and ended in a player ring-out. Do not treat the crank as a verified stable movement option; motor/phase/tuning and representative full-battle testing remain open. Standard wheel-driven auto-combat passed separately.
- **Generated-audio permission review:** the five sounds were generated for this project and their provenance is listed in `LICENSES/ASSET_LICENSES.txt`. The sound generator service’s current commercial and redistribution terms were not independently audited; confirm those terms before commercial/store distribution.
- **Weapon specificity:** Drill and Rotary Saw use rotating procedural graphics plus repeated contact sensors, not standalone simulated rigid-body cutting/blade assemblies. The Hammer head is a jointed rigid body with a damage sensor; the Rocket Launcher spawns physical projectiles with impact and local splash behavior.
- **Armor interpretation:** armor modules are physical, joint-attached bodies with per-module durability, contact targeting, limited chassis-damage mitigation while plates remain active, and collision disabled on destruction. This is not a detailed directional/angle-based deflection simulation. The GDD's Rammer calls for a front plow; the current Bulwark socket model exposes an `upper_armor` socket but no dedicated front-armor socket, so the authored legal build can put AR-01 at an upper armor mount instead. This is a design/attachment mismatch, not silently treated as fully matching the requested front-plow placement.
- **AI/content playtest:** all 12 opponent configurations and their data/unlock validity passed bounded headless startup. Archetype policies vary pursuit/coasting behavior while sharing movement, damage and health rules. The complete set of 12 fights was not individually observed to victory/defeat on a rendered device; extensive difficulty balance and deterministic replay testing remain open.
- **Local-save migration/recovery:** atomic temp/backup and malformed-current fallback paths exist, but a separate-process relaunch and a deliberate corrupted-save restoration test were not run in this milestone.
- **Full production checklist:** UI transitions, builder, inventory states, campaign data/progression rules, core combat effects/SFX and the wheel-driven automatic battle are implemented or headlessly exercised to the limits recorded in `TEST_REPORT.md`. Stable crank-leg locomotion, polished six-step callouts, rendered result-card/particle QA, full audio set, final mix, rights review and all checklist animation states remain to be verified or produced.

## Present behavior (not issues)

- The profile is local/offline; no account, network service, ads or cloud saves are present.
- A fresh profile starts with a basic wheel and Drill. Campaign first-clear rewards unlock equipment bundles; repeat victories do not award the same one-time scrap or equipment bundle again. The headless smoke test simulates a fresh ownership state and restores the test profile afterwards.
- Component rotation is saved per mounted socket and applied when constructing combat equipment: front weapon sensors/hammer/rocket direction, armor and utility rigid bodies, wheel visual phase, Booster impulse direction and the physical crank-leg phase use the configured angle. The JSON save/load round-trip and player/AI arena transforms passed headless regression assertions; rendered orientation on a handset remains Not tested.
- Combat audio/VFX is event-driven: hit/damage calls spawn CPU spark/smoke bursts and metal impact playback; rocket launch adds a muzzle burst/SFX and a physical projectile trail; projectile impact spawns a blast/SFX; component disable adds a scrap burst; each vehicle gets a speed-modulated engine loop, equipped rotary-tool loop and exhaust particles. SFX volume is taken from saved settings; battle end stops loops. Headless event and resource checks passed; audible quality/rendered appearance are Not tested.
- The campaign has twelve nodes in three chapters, with battles 4/8/12 marked as bosses, five data-authored archetypes, two arena identifiers, and a level-5 final boss build. Both arena setups use local physics geometry; Scrap Pit adds symmetric static side ramps. No moving/random hazards are present.
- The project is openable/editable in Godot 4.7.2. This statement does not imply Android compatibility or exported-device verification.


## Additional explicit GDD interaction/animation gaps

The Garage implements tap-to-select/tap-to-place, not drag-and-drop. The main-menu featured machine is a static procedural illustration rather than an idle-animation loop. Tutorial text points to relevant real features, but the finished animated pointer/callout artwork remains pending.
