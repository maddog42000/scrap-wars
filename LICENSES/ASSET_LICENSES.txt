SCRAP WARS asset provenance and license notes

VISUALS
Machine shapes, arena art, UI styling, projectile visuals, and particle textures are authored procedurally in GDScript for this project. No stock images, downloaded sprite sheets, or third-party particle textures are bundled.

FONT
DejaVu Sans TTF is bundled at assets/fonts/DejaVuSans.ttf. Its copyright/license terms are included in DejaVu-Fonts-LICENSE.txt; consult that license file for the applicable use and redistribution conditions.

GENERATED AUDIO

- assets/audio/rotary_tool_loop.mp3 — looping powered rotary cutter/drill texture.
- assets/audio/metal_impact.mp3 — metal weapon/module impact.

No separate composer/stock-audio license document was supplied for generated outputs.
